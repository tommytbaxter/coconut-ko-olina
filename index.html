A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before you arrive.

How do I arrange a reservation?

Contact Garrett at Ko Olina Rentals with your dates and number of guests. Management handles availability, quotes, reservations, payments, and guest support.

How long can I stay?

The home is marketed for extended stays. Please ask Garrett to confirm the applicable minimum stay and terms for your dates. Final rental information will be added after management review.

What should I know about parking?

The home has garage parking. Vehicle size restrictions apply; ask management to confirm clearance and fit before choosing a rental vehicle.

Are pets or smoking permitted?

The existing rental information lists no pets and no smoking. Confirm all current house rules with management before booking.
